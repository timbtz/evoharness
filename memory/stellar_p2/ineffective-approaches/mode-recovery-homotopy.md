## Why it failed
The mode-recovery line segment physically traces backward into the truncated high-mode structure. Because the full bank seed camps exactly on the QI constraint wall (`log10_qi ≈ -3.99`), recovering its modes mathematically pulls the escaped boundary back into the wall. Simultaneously, any backward interpolation `t > 0.5` crosses back inside the `1e-3` novelty ball. This creates an inescapable trap: the L gains are perfectly negated by the novelty/QI penalties, yielding an unsubmitbable near-copy. Negative contractions applied to escapes yield the exact same trap.

## Verdict
