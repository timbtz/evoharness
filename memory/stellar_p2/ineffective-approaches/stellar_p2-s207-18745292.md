## Why it failed
Writers predicted that deterministic structural depth contraction (the proven mechanism on public seeds) would lower the violation of independent NAE seeds from ~0.50 down to feasible levels. The code applied these batched grids. However, dynamically generated NAE/ellipse seeds start at enormous baseline violations (>0.5). Applying structural contractions (which are designed to push the aspect ratio to the tolerance wall) to boundaries that are already massively violating the aspect ratio constraint strictly increases the violation, driving scores deeper into the negative.
