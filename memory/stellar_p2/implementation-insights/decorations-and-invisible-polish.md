## Why it failed
At `stellar_p2-s101-20239089` and `stellar_p2-s102-48117936`, the post-escape polish loop accepts exactly **zero moves** due to two compounding positive-feedback loops:
1. **Invisible Mutations:** `_mutate` steps have amplitude `sigma · ref · 1.5^-(m+n)`. With `SIGMA0 = 0.0012` and `ref ≈ 0.1`, perturbations are ~1e-4 on the lowest modes—exact "epsilon dust" that train fidelity cannot see. Even when `SIGMA0` was cranked to 0.005 (c0014) and low-mode floors were added (c0012f), the **escape boundary is a strict local L-optimum**. Any visible mutation strictly lowers L, and an L-maximizing acceptance key strictly rejects it.
2. **Death Spiral:** Invisible/losing mutations return scores that fail `scores[i] > best_key`. The loop misreads this as a stall and shrinks `sigma *= SHRINK`. Smaller sigma guarantees deeper invisibility, forming a closed positive-feedback loop. No acceptance key tweak, return gate modification, or mutation restructuring can break this local optimum trap.
3. **Wall-Clock Guards:** In high-mode bank seed regimes, vlf evals cost 12-27s. A budget reserve of `LF_FINALISTS * 2.2 * teval` can exceed the remaining time after just one batch, starving Phase 0 of supply and skipping the LF verification entirely.

## Verdict
recurring pitfall — If you score exactly the same as the incumbent to 4+ decimals, verify the mechanism actually fires! Specifically: check if polish steps are simulator-visible (amplitude > 1e-4 on low modes), ensure triggers aren't gated too strictly, and validate that wall-clock budget reserves (`reserve()`) don't prematurely kill Phase 0 supply.
